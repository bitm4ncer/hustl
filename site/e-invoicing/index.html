Deadlines

Who has to do what, and when.

The rules apply to domestic B2B invoices in Germany. The date that matters for issuing depends on your prior-year turnover.

FromObligationWho
1 Jan 2025Receive e-invoicesAll domestic B2B businesses
1 Jan 2027Issue e-invoicesPrior-year turnover above €800,000
1 Jan 2028Issue e-invoicesAll businesses, except small businesses under § 19 UStG
OngoingReceive & archiveAlso small businesses under § 19 UStG

Important: Small businesses under § 19 UStG are exempt from issuing e-invoices, but must be able to receive and archive them. Small-amount invoices up to €250 and travel tickets remain exceptions. E-invoices must also be archived electronically (generally 8 years). This page is general information as of November 2026, not tax or legal advice. Check your individual case with your tax adviser.

Formats

A PDF alone is not an e-invoice.

An e-invoice is a structured, machine-readable dataset according to EN 16931. The two common formats in Germany are XRechnung and ZUGFeRD.

A PDF is not enough

A normal PDF invoice is readable by humans, but not machine-readable. It does not fulfil the structured e-invoice requirement.

XRechnung

Pure XML according to EN 16931. Common for public-sector clients. You need a viewer or software to read it.

ZUGFeRD / Factur-X

A hybrid: a human-readable PDF/A-3 file with an embedded XML dataset. Hustl creates this format.

FAQ

E-invoicing questions, answered.

When do I have to issue e-invoices in Germany?

Since 1 January 2025, domestic B2B businesses must be able to receive e-invoices. From 1 January 2027, businesses with a prior-year turnover above €800,000 must issue them. From 1 January 2028, all businesses must issue them, except small businesses under § 19 UStG.

Do small businesses have to issue e-invoices?

No. Small businesses under § 19 UStG are permanently exempt from issuing e-invoices, but they must be able to receive and archive them. They may issue e-invoices voluntarily.

Is a PDF invoice enough?

No. An e-invoice is a structured, machine-readable dataset according to EN 16931, such as XRechnung (XML) or ZUGFeRD (PDF/A-3 with embedded XML). A plain PDF is not an e-invoice.

What is the difference between XRechnung and ZUGFeRD?

XRechnung is pure XML. ZUGFeRD/Factur-X is a hybrid: a human-readable PDF/A-3 with an embedded XML dataset. Hustl creates ZUGFeRD/Factur-X invoices.

Does Hustl support XRechnung?

Hustl focuses on ZUGFeRD/Factur-X (hybrid PDF/A-3). XRechnung is a pure XML format and is not the core output. If you need XRechnung for public-sector clients, check your workflow before relying on Hustl.

Can I use Hustl for my tax return?

Hustl helps you collect receipts, organize your finances and export data. It is not tax advice and does not file tax returns. Validate e-invoices with the official KoSIT validator and review your process with your tax adviser.

Can I self-host Hustl?

Yes. Hustl is open source (ISC) and runs on your own server. The standalone app runs locally without a server.

What can I export?

PDFs, CSV tables and a full archive with the database and documents. That gives your tax adviser the underlying data without locking you into a cloud platform.

Is this deadline information legally binding?

No. This page is general information, last updated November 2026, not tax or legal advice. Check your individual case with your tax adviser.
